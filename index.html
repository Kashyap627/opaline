Logic settings

One-shot calls capi.y2jar.cc/scr/{videoId} and reads downloadUrl straight from the JSON. Legacy walks the older st/a token flow.

Audio-only plays the resolved MP3 natively — lighter, pure music. If the link expires mid-listen, Opaline falls back to YouTube at the same position. Switching reloads the track.
